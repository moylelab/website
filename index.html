About the Moyle Lab

We study the genetics and genomics of adaptation and speciation, to understand the mechanisms that create new trait variation and new species. Using both model and non-model systems, we are interested in how organisms diversify, how ecological interactions shape evolutionary trajectories, and how new species arise in the face of ongoing gene flow and environmental change.

Within the broad themes of adaptation and speciation, lab members work on topics ranging from molecular mechanisms of species recognition through to the ecology and genetics of reproductive isolation, trait evolution, and diversification across wild systems.
